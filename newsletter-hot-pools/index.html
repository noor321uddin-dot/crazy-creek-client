The Pools

The Contrast Loop

 

Move from the 40°C thermal soak to the 8°C cold plunge and back. Hot opens you up, cold resets you — the recovery cycle athletes and hikers drive here for.

Good to know: one of the four pools is seasonal (open May–October). The thermal soak and cold plunge run every day of winter, 9am–10pm — snow or shine.

The Science

Hot Soaks, Cold Plunge

Immune system. Regular cold exposure helps strengthen the body's natural defences over time.

Less inflammation. The cold plunge constricts blood vessels, flushing lactic acid and easing muscle soreness.

Stress & calm. Cold immersion triggers an endorphin release — a runner's high in about three minutes.

Faster recovery. Athletes and hikers use the hot-cold contrast cycle to bounce back quicker — soak hot, plunge cold, repeat.
